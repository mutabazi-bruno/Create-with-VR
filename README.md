# Create-with-VR

My VR room from Unity Learn's **Create with VR** course, built in Unity 6 with the XR Interaction Toolkit and tested on a Meta Quest 2.

## What's in the room

- Modern living room with a city view, a working wall clock and three public domain paintings you can move between hooks
- Teleporting, snap and smooth turning, and an optional continuous movement setting
- Things to pick up and use: tennis ball and racket, fruit, hats you can try on in the mirror, a TV remote, phone, flashlight, lighter and candle, dart gun, polaroid camera, watering can and a cabinet with opening doors
- Spatial audio, controller haptics, a record player and closed captions
- Welcome, settings and reset menus
- Baked lighting with light probes and a reflection probe

The course challenge scenes (Architecture, 3D Painting and Training) are fixed in `Assets/Challenges`.

## Open it

1. Open the folder in Unity Hub with **Unity 6000.0.80f1** (Android build support).
2. Open `Assets/Scenes/Bruno Room.unity`.
3. Build to a Quest from **File > Build Profiles** (Android).

The completion certificate is in the `Certificate` folder.
